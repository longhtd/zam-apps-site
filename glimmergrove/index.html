About the game

Glimmer Grove is a cozy idle garden game set under the stars. Your tiny sprout friend Pip looks after glowing plants even when you are not playing.

Come back to a garden that has grown, collect dew drops and welcome new spirits.

How to play

  1. Tap Play to visit the garden and meet Pip.
  2. Water and tend the glowing plants with dew.
  3. Leave the garden and come back later to find it has grown.
  4. Spend what you collect to grow the grove and invite new spirits.
