About the game

Snug Cats is a cozy logic puzzle on a patchwork quilt. Place one cat in every row, every column and every coloured blanket, and make sure no two cats touch.

Finish each puzzle and watch the cats settle in for a nap.

How to play

  1. Tap a square to place a cat.
  2. Put exactly one cat in every row, column and blanket.
  3. Cats may never touch each other, even diagonally.
  4. Solve the puzzle and the cats curl up to nap.
