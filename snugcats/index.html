About the game

Every cat needs a cushion. Snug Cats is a calm logic puzzle where you place exactly one cat in every row, every column and every coloured blanket, and keep them apart, even diagonally.

All 400 puzzles are built so you can solve them by logic alone. Stuck? The hint paw explains the technique instead of just giving the answer.

Finish a puzzle and the whole family curls up for a Nap Moment.

How to play

  1. 1

    Read the blankets

    The board is split into coloured blanket regions.

  2. 2

    Place one cat in each

    Every row, every column and every blanket needs exactly one cat.

  3. 3

    Keep cats apart

    No two cats may touch, not even diagonally.

  4. 4

    Tap to cross, hold to place

    Tap to cross out a square. Long-press or double-tap to place a cat. Drag to cross a line.

  5. 5

    Use the hint paw

    It highlights squares and explains the technique. Three free hints per day.

  6. 6

    Learn in Nap School

    Short lessons teach the tricks, step by step.

  7. 7

    Settle in

    Solve the puzzle and the cats curl up for a Nap Moment.
