About the game

Word Lodge is a relaxing word puzzle game that blends classic word-connect swiping with satisfying crossword-style fill-ins. No timers, no life-limit stress: just you, a crackling fireplace and a warm autumn view waiting to be uncovered.

Every puzzle you solve reveals another piece of a hand-illustrated autumn mountain retreat, so your cozy hideaway comes to life level by level.

How to play

  1. Swipe across the letter wheel to connect letters and spell words.
  2. Watch each word lock into the crossword grid. Words overlap and share letters, so every discovery opens new possibilities.
  3. Stuck? Tap Shuffle to rearrange the letters, or use a Hint to reveal a tricky spot.
  4. Unlimited tries on every puzzle. Take your time, there is no penalty for guessing.
