About the game

Word Lodge blends swipe-to-spell word connect with satisfying crossword fill-ins. Swipe across the letter wheel, watch each word lock into the grid, and let the fire crackle while you think.

Every finished level uncovers more of a hand-painted autumn world. Meet nine forest companions, open milestone chests and come back each day for a fresh Daily Puzzle that is the same for everyone.

It is built for calm: no countdowns, no lives to lose, and rewarded videos only when you choose to watch one.

How to play

  1. 1

    Swipe the letters

    Slide your finger across the circular letter wheel to spell a word, then lift to submit.

  2. 2

    Fill the crossword

    Valid words lock into the grid. Words cross and share letters, so each answer helps the next.

  3. 3

    Collect bonus words

    Words that are not in the grid still count as bonus words and pay coins.

  4. 4

    Shuffle or use a hint

    Shuffle reorders the wheel. A hint costs 100 coins and reveals a letter, and you can also watch a video for one.

  5. 5

    Uncover the scenery

    Every finished level pays coins and reveals more of the painted world.

  6. 6

    Open chests

    Every ten levels a milestone chest opens, and each world ends with a trophy.

  7. 7

    Come back daily

    Claim the daily reward, play the Daily Puzzle and finish quests to keep your streak.

  8. 8

    Meet the companions

    Unlock forest friends as you level up. Each gives a perk such as bonus coins or a free daily hint.
