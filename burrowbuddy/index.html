About the game

Burrow Buddy is a cozy pet-care game. Choose a buddy to live in your burrow, then keep it fed, clean, rested and happy.

Each buddy has its own personality, favourite foods and mini-games.

How to play

  1. Choose the buddy who will live in your burrow.
  2. Feed, wash and put your buddy to bed to keep every meter full.
  3. Play mini-games together to earn coins.
  4. Spend coins on food and care to make the burrow a nicer home.
