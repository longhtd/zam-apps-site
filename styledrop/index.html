About the game

StyleDrop is a fashion styling game. Choose a model, pick pieces from your wardrobe and dress her for each themed challenge.

Your look is scored, and the stars you earn unlock the next challenge.

How to play

  1. Choose your model; everyone you style wears the same wardrobe.
  2. Open the wardrobe and mix pieces to match the challenge theme.
  3. Submit your look and see how it scores.
  4. Collect stars and rewards to unlock the next challenge.
