About the game

Pixel Picnic is a colour-sorting puzzle on a picnic blanket. Hungry ant swarms nibble a pixel picture one colour at a time.

Plan the order, send the right ants and watch the picture disappear.

How to play

  1. Look at the pixel picture on the blanket.
  2. Send ant swarms to eat the pixels, one colour at a time.
  3. Clear the whole picture to finish the level.
  4. Finish daily levels and quests for gifts.
