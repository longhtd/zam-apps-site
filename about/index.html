About

Calm apps for everyday moments.

Zamverse is an independent studio making simple, beautiful apps and cozy games.

Zamverse is a small independent studio from Vietnam. We build calm apps and cozy games for phones, with one idea in mind: software should feel like a deep breath, not a chore.

Our puzzle games live on games.zamverse.com. This site is home to our apps.

We read every email. If something is unclear, broken or could simply be nicer, tell us and we will make it better.

What we care about

Easy to start

Every app explains itself in seconds. No long tutorials and no complicated menus.

Made for short moments

Use it for two minutes in a queue or an hour on the sofa. Your progress stays on your device.

Warm and handmade

Quiet visuals and small details you will want to come back to.

Respectful by design

We collect as little data as we can and explain it plainly in each app's privacy policy.
